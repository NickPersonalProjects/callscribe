<p><b>CallScribe</b> records carrier phone calls through Shizuku and transcribes them entirely on your Android device.</p>

<p><b>Features</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Non-root recording of incoming and outgoing carrier calls</li>
<li>On-device NVIDIA Parakeet transcription through sherpa-onnx</li>
<li>Searchable transcripts with timestamps and speaker labels</li>
<li>Audio playback synchronized to transcript segments</li>
<li>App-private recording storage and configurable retention</li>
<li>TXT, Markdown, SRT, and audio sharing</li>
<li>No advertising, analytics, or cloud speech service</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Requirements</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Android 12 or newer</li>
<li><a href="https://github.com/thedjchi/Shizuku">Shizuku</a>; the thedjchi fork is recommended</li>
<li>Approximately 800 MB free storage for the default speech model and download staging</li>
</ul>

<p>The internet permission is used only to download user-selected speech models. Call audio and transcripts are not uploaded.</p>
